Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal assistant operated by Paul Fost for his own use only.

Data accessed

With explicit authorization, Hermes Agent may access Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive data belonging to the authorizing account, solely to perform tasks requested by that account's owner (for example: summarizing email, checking calendar events, and storing encrypted backups in Drive).

Use and sharing

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, except the AI model provider that processes requests on the owner's behalf.

Storage and deletion

Credentials and data are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
